01 / ABOUT ME

A life without invisible walls.

I’m Ranvijay Rathore, a first-year student in IIM Bangalore’s online Digital Business Entrepreneurship programme, and a self-taught creator who doesn’t want to pick just one thing.

I’ve broken away from society’s chains. My goal isn’t money or fame. It’s to enjoy life fully, live truly free, and make myself proud. I want to be everything. Do everything. Build a life that feels like mine.

Minecraft was my first sandbox: anything, anytime, no restrictions. That freedom shaped how I think. War Robots is my favourite game and will always hold a special place. I’m learning to live with Luffy’s freedom and Thorfinn’s peace.

Other people’s opinions don’t get to decide who I become. In the end, what matters is what I think of myself.

“I have no enemies”
